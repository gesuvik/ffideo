# FFideo privacy policy — provisional draft

**Status:** owner and legal review required before public sale.

**Effective date:** not set.

**Operator and privacy contact:** to be supplied before publication.

## Local media processing

FFideo processes selected media files locally on the Mac. The application does not upload media content for conversion, does not provide cloud processing, and does not include analytics or advertising in this release candidate.

FFideo reads only files the user selects and writes converted results to the chosen local destination. Diagnostic information is kept local unless the user intentionally copies or sends it to support. Diagnostics should avoid media contents and full paths unless the user explicitly chooses to include them.

## Trial and local settings

FFideo stores the number of successful evaluation exports and application preferences locally. Failed, cancelled, incompatible, or discarded conversions do not consume an evaluation export.

## Purchase and activation

The local release candidate does not contact a payment or activation service. A future commercial build is expected to use Polar for checkout and license activation. When the user chooses those actions, Polar and ordinary internet infrastructure may process information such as payment/contact details, IP address, license key, product identifier, activation identifier, device-scoped information, and security logs under Polar's own notices and the final FFideo configuration.

FFideo must not send media content, media filenames, or conversion history to Polar for licensing.

## Support

If a user contacts support, the operator may process the message, contact details, purchase evidence, license information, and diagnostics the user elects to provide. Retention and deletion periods must be finalized before public launch.

## Sharing, retention, and rights

FFideo does not sell personal data. Data may be handled by the approved payment/licensing provider, hosting provider, email provider, or authorities when legally required. The final policy must identify the operator, processors, international-transfer basis, retention periods, rights-request channel, and launch jurisdictions.

## Children, security, and changes

FFideo is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no software or storage system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Material policy changes must receive a new effective date and be published with the release.
