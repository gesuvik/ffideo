Refund policy

The proposed voluntary refund terms for a future FFideo purchase.

Status: Owner and Merchant of Record configuration approval required before public sale.

Proposed voluntary policy

The proposed voluntary policy is a full refund on request within 14 calendar days of purchase. The purchaser should provide the order identifier and enough information to locate the transaction. A refund request does not require uploading media files.

Processing

Refunds will be processed through the approved Merchant of Record. Processing time, exchange-rate differences, taxes, payment-method rules, chargebacks, and any provider fees are governed by the provider and applicable law.

Consumer rights

This policy does not limit mandatory consumer rights. The operator may address abusive or repeated purchase/refund patterns, but must not deny rights that cannot legally be waived.

Offline activation

An already activated offline installation may continue to function after a refund in version 1.0; FFideo does not promise remote revocation. The final checkout and support pages must disclose the approved policy and contact before sale.
